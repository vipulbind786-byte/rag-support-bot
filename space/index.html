Offline RAG Support Bot — live demo

Hybrid retrieval running 100% in your browser: chunking → BM25 + TF-IDF → RRF fusion → relevance gate → citations. No server, no API, $0. Production build runs the same pipeline with local embeddings + a cross-encoder reranker on your own machine.
line-aware chunks (invoice rows never split), 20-line overlap tail BM25 exact match TF-IDF distinctive terms RRF voting fusion coverage gate → abstain, never guess
hit@5 = fraction of golden questions whose answer appears in the top-5 cited chunks. Off-topic probes must return zero citations — the bot abstains instead of hallucinating.
Why this matters for a support bot: retrieval is deterministic and auditable (every answer carries the exact sentence + source), the gate makes the bot say "escalate to a human" when the corpus can't answer, and the whole thing runs on a laptop with no cloud — right for hospitals, legal, and finance where data cannot leave the premises.
