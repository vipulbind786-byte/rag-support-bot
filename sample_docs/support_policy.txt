[page 1] ACME Retail Support Policy — Version 4.2

Refund Policy
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Partial refunds are issued for opened items at 50% of the price. Refunds are processed within 3 business days and credited to the original payment method. Refunds for digital products are not available after download.

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within India. Express shipping is 2-3 business days for an additional fee of Rs 150. Free shipping applies to orders above Rs 1000. International shipping takes 10-14 business days.

Returns for Damaged Items
Damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Return is immediate and a replacement is shipped within 2 business days. Photographs of the damage are required for the claim.

Payment Methods
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, and net banking. EMI options are available on orders above Rs 5000.

Contact and Support
Support email: support@acmeretail.example. Live chat is available from 9 to 6, Monday to Friday. Office hours are 9 to 6 IST. Our headquarters is located in Pune, Maharashtra.

Password and Account
To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the emailed link. Link expires in 24 hours. Account deletion requests are processed in 7 days.

Warranty
All electronics carry a 1 year manufacturer warranty. Warranty claims require the invoice number. Extended warranty of 2 years is available for Rs 499.

[page 2] Escalation and Refund Disputes
If a refund is not processed within 3 business days, escalate to the supervisor queue via support ticket with the order ID. Disputes above Rs 10000 are reviewed by the finance team within 5 business days. Chargeback claims must include bank statement references.
